# LivingHUD: notes for Claude

A Solo Leveling-style RPG HUD for real life. Real tasks are quests, progress shows as levels and stats, and quests pay real dollars toward real rewards.

- **Full design doc:** https://claude.ai/artifact/WqMKCWp6aLyHbD3nHLb5S2 (read it with the docs tools before planning a new phase)
- **Planning session:** https://claude.ai/code/session_01PQeFGVB85UubwfeCcxCbVN

## About the owner
- New to coding: explain steps in plain language and keep setup minimal.
- Starts as a personal tool but will become a public product, so build with scaling in mind (accounts, sync, multi-user).

## Decisions already made
- **Stack:** React + TypeScript + Vite as an installable PWA, hosted on Vercel (every merge to `main` deploys). IndexedDB via Dexie is the working copy; native wrapper later.
- **Cloud sync (owner wants no extra services, so no Supabase):** `api/sync.ts` is a Vercel Function that stores the whole save in the project's Vercel Blob store. A secret 25-character sync code links devices; the server only stores its SHA-256 hash. `src/db/sync.ts` uploads ~1.5s after any change and pulls on open/focus; the device with unsynced changes wins. Real logins come later (Phase 5) on the same Vercel storage.
- **Architecture:** game rules live only in `src/engine/` (no React, no DB). Every action emits an event on the bus in `src/engine/events.ts`; screens, and later smart glasses and the AI System, only listen.
- **Data:** totals are derived from the completion log, never stored. Every record carries `userId` and timestamps.
- **Stats:** STR (fitness/posture), VIT (sleep/water/nutrition), INT (learning/reflection), CHA (friends/family/dating), AGI (discipline/life admin), WLT (money/career/side income). Each is a 0–100 score over the last 30 days and can go down.
- **Quests:** daily, side (weekly), main (multi-stage). Sleep (`vit-sleep`, 7.5h, min 6.5h) and Water + vitamins (`vit-water`) are separate dailies; the old combined `vit-trio` is split on startup and after any save import (`splitVitTrio`: the old quest becomes Water + vitamins, keeping its history, and a Sleep daily with fixed id `quest-vit-sleep` is added). Each has a "minimum version" for bad days that pays half and keeps the streak.
- **Rewards in dollars, no gold:** a monthly budget ($150 default) that about 80% completion earns in full. Overflow goes to the Vault, the only way to buy A/S rewards. The wallet unlocks only after the month's savings goal is met. Rewards are ranked E–S by price: E <$10, D $10–30, C $30–75, B $75–200, A $200–500, S $500+, each with a minimum level.
- **Shop extras:** `REWARD_IDEAS` (`src/engine/library.ts`) is a personalised idea catalog (training/posture gear, desk setup, NFC/MacroDroid, health, learning, side income, social, gaming, smart glasses) shown in the Shop with one-tap add, your stats first; prices are typical, editable. Every reward has Edit; "Buy something else" is a one-off purchase through the same gates (`buyOneOff`, optionally kept in the shop).
- **Plans (raves, festivals, trips; Asia is the biggest reward):** owner chose "money is the gate". `plans` + `planFunds` tables, rules in `src/engine/plans.ts`. Money moved from the Vault into a plan is out of the free Vault (`wallet.inPlans`); any cost line can be booked once the plan's fund covers it and the month's savings goal is met, with no level gate (a Purchase with `planId`, paid `fromVault`, offset by a negative fund entry). Rave/festival tickets get their Recovery Day after the event (`forDay`). Templates: Rave, Festival weekend, Asia trip (~$3,240, typical not quoted). Optional "Expedition" main quest: save-25/50/75/100 stages clear themselves (`advancePlanQuest`). Pace uses `wallet.perMonth` (average Vault growth over the last 3 full months).
- **Weighted daily pay:** dailies split the monthly pot by shares (`DAILY_WEIGHTS` in `src/engine/economy.ts`): the strength + posture routine is 3 shares (it takes the longest), everything else 1. The pot doesn't grow, so ~80% completion still fills the budget; XP stays 20 per daily. The HUD shows each daily's pay; past completions keep what they paid.
- **Finance tracker (feature `finance`):** real money, never game dollars. `transactions` (expense/income/'nospend' marker, category, note, optional `billId`) + `bills` (weekly/monthly/yearly, `nextDue`) tables, in the save; `player.finance` = {limit, billsInLimit, since}. Rules in `src/engine/finance.ts`: categories Needs/Wants/Savings/Other (Savings never counts as spending; bills count unless switched off) and income Pay/Side/Other; `totals`, `monthPace` (even-pace expected, left, per-day left, under/near/over), `weekFinance` (weekly pace = limit×12/52), `monthReport` (by category vs last month, top 5, kept rate, daily average, bills/month, grade S–E, one tip), `nextDue`/`billsDue`. XP only (`financeXp`, in the summary): +15 per finished Mon–Sun week at/under weekly pace with ≥1 entry, +50 per finished month under the limit with ≥5 entries, counted from `since`. Owner chose: quick manual entry only (no CSV/notifications), shorter category list, Shop purchases NOT mirrored, no savings-gate link. Writes in `src/db/finance.ts`; any entry today clears `wlt-spend` ("Log today's spending"); "No spend today" (today/yesterday) clears `side-wlt-nospend`; "Done reviewing" on the weekly check-in clears `side-wlt-money`, on the monthly report clears `offer-budget-review`; paying a bill logs it and rolls the due date. UI: HUD Money panel (`src/ui/finance/MoneyPanel.tsx`: pace bar, quick add, bills due ≤3 days), Finances screen (`Finance.tsx`: Overview + limit, Entries by month with edit, Bills, Weekly check-in, Monthly report), PC sidebar "Finances", money lines in the weekly report, activity feed 'money'.
- **Incentives (features `incentives` and `penaltyQuest`):** rules in `src/engine/incentives.ts` (pure, tested), derived from the completion log, never stored. Rewards: combo meter (each perfect day in a row +×0.1 quest XP, max ×2, applied in `completeQuest`; missing a day resets, Recovery Days pause), perfect-day chest (+25 XP in the summary plus a Rare+ card, `rewardPerfectDay`), 3-card packs at combo 3/7/14/30 (first Epic+), secret card series unlocked by best combo (Eclipse 7, Zenith 30; `SECRET_SERIES` in loot.ts, excluded from drops until unlocked), Shields (one per perfect Mon–Sun week) that cancel a Penalty Quest. Penalty: missing ≥ `player.incentives.threshold` (1–5, default 2) dailies opens a Penalty Quest the next day, a seeded pick from `player.incentives.menu` (the owner's least-wanted chores, editable). While open the HUD turns red (`data-penalty` on `<html>`), and if `lockShop` the Shop, one-off buys and plan bookings are refused (`shopLock` → `ShopContext.locked`). Cleared by "I did it" (honour) or a Shield → `penaltyClears` table (in the save). Owner chose these over more XP loss and tunes it in Settings (`IncentiveSettings.tsx`). Phone game lock: `api/trigger.ts?t=TOKEN&status=1` returns `{locked, task}`; the app POSTs `setstatus` only when it changes (`pushPenaltyStatus`); MacroDroid steps in Settings (app launched → HTTP GET → if contains `"locked":true` → home screen).
- **Decluttered phone layout:** tabs are Today, Quests, Log, Shop, More. More (`src/ui/More.tsx`) holds Party, Finances, Collection, Plans, Journal, Top 3 history, Focus, Weekly report, Workout, Customize HUD, Settings (as screens with Back). The HUD is a panel registry: alerts (Penalty Zone, surprise, recovery, routine offer, bonds) always on top, then panels per the per-device layout (`src/ui/hudLayout.ts`, localStorage: order + Top/More/Hidden; default top = status, dailies, combo, top 3, today, money; the rest fold into a "More" `<details>`). The PC dashboard shows every non-hidden panel unfolded.
- **Stat names spelled out:** the owner doesn't know the acronyms, so UI shows `statName(key)` (Strength, Vitality, Intelligence, Charisma, Agility, Wealth); the short keys stay internal only.
- **Special rules:** dates cost wallet money and also earn CHA XP. Buying a rave or big night out grants a Recovery Day token.
- **Phase 2 rulings:** E–B rewards spend wallet then Vault; A/S are Vault-only; S also needs one cleared main quest. Unspent wallet money rolls into the Vault when the month ends. Side quests pay $10 (budget/15) and 40 XP, reset Mondays; main quest stages pay their template amount scaled to budget/150 and have no minimum version. Side/main pay is on top of dailies, so it mostly lands in the Vault. Purchases can be refunded the same game day.
- **Live tracking, no paid AI (owner is on Android):** the strength routine has a camera rep counter. `src/capture/pose.ts` runs Google MediaPipe pose tracking on-device (engine wasm bundled from the npm package, model in `public/models`, nothing sent anywhere); `src/engine/workout.ts` turns body points into reps/plank seconds, so glasses can feed the same counter later. Sets are logged to the `sets` table (split across the day) and the quest auto-completes when every target is hit (and un-clears if a correction drops below). Training is `player.plan` (`TrainingPlan`): a cycle (default Push, Pull, Legs, Push, Pull, Rest; legs and rest alternate) plus a daily posture routine for forward head posture and anterior pelvic tilt (chin tucks, wall slides, prone Y, chest and neck stretches, hip flexor stretch with posterior tilt, glute bridges, dead bugs). `planDay` walks from `startedOn`: trained days and rest days advance the cycle, missed workouts wait. Without a saved plan it's a default from the fitness test; an older `player.routine` shows as a single 'full' day with a switch offer. Posture research (owner-approved, Oct 2026): stretching alone doesn't change posture (Warneke 2024 meta-analysis); strengthening helps neck/upper back; pelvic tilt responds to glutes plus awareness/motor control. So posture also has pelvic tilt practice, split-stance tuck holds and daily posture check-ins (logged by MacroDroid reminder taps via `api/trigger.ts?x=postureCheckins`), plus side quests for incline walking and a weekly posture scan. `src/engine/posture.ts` + `src/ui/PostureScan.tsx`: side-on head angle (ear over shoulder, a CVA stand-in) and peak walking hip extension, saved to the `scans` table; trend-only, not clinical. Camera points go through `trueProportions` before any angle maths. Custom exercises are manual; holds have a timer; "Fix today's count" logs a `correction` set (honour system). Progression per the design doc: after 21 full-routine days in a row (Recovery Days skip) the HUD offers ~+25% targets; Accept/Not now both set `routineReviewedOn` and restart the 3-week count. Nothing changes without the player accepting. Auto-complete links: `api/trigger.ts` queues `{questId, version, at}` in one inbox blob per player (hash of `settings.triggerToken`, version-checked writes via `server/blob.ts`); `src/db/triggers.ts` applies them on open/focus and every 2 min, counting each for the game day it happened. Settings has the links and MacroDroid steps. Photo/AI checks wait until the owner wants AI costs. Watch Vercel Blob operation counts on the free plan.
- **Feature switches:** `player.features` (missing = on), listed in `FEATURES` (`src/engine/party.ts`) and shown in Settings. New optional features go behind one; switching off hides UI, never deletes records. Older code opens newer Dexie versions fine (tested), so rollbacks are safe.
- **New XP sources pay XP only, not dollars** (keeps the $150 budget tuned to dailies).
- **Quest offers:** `src/engine/offers.ts` + `src/ui/QuestOffers.tsx` at the top of the Quests board. Every 3 days (from signup) a seeded set of side quests: 3 "For you" (owner-specific: posture/APT/FHP, pull-ups, raves/festivals, Asia trip, side income, MacroDroid, gaming), 2 of them from the two weakest stats; 2 "Better you" habits; 1 "Dark side (for fun)" (harmless narcissism/Machiavellianism: main character day, negotiator, three moves ahead…), 2 every other window. Every Monday two big main quests (months long, bigger stage pay) from different stats, weakest first. Seed = `surpriseSeed(player)` + window, scores from the day before the window (so sets don't reshuffle mid-window); nothing stored until added (templateId = offer id; quests added before the window are skipped, ones added during it show ADDED). Briefing mentions new offers. The Shop's "Ideas for you" window rotates the same way (`rewardOffers`, same 3-day window, 6 picks: 2 E/D, 2 C/B, 1 A/S, rest for your stats; 54 ideas in `REWARD_IDEAS`) with "Browse all"; ☆ stars an idea into `player.favoriteRewards` (synced), pinned on top as "Working toward" with `goalProgress` (money toward the price, wallet+Vault for E–B, Vault for A/S, plus level/stat/main-quest/savings blockers); starred goals also show in the PC Treasury panel.
- **Party list (Phase 4, done):** `people` + `contacts` tables. Message/call/hangout/date earn 5/15/30/40 CHA XP; any contact clears "Reach out", a hangout clears the hangout side quest, a call with family clears "Call family". Bond warning after `bondDays` (21, family 14) without contact, on the HUD and Party tab.
- **Focus dungeons and bosses (Phase 4, done):** `focusRuns` + `bosses` tables. A session earns 1 XP/min (+10 for a full clear); a linked daily/side quest clears at 30+ min (min at 10+), not if a strict session broke. The running session lives in localStorage (`src/db/focus.ts`, survives reloads); relaxed by default, strict breaks after 60s away (`startFocusWatch` is app-wide). Bosses: HP = 15-min hits; damage from linked sessions or a manual "Hit"; defeat = +100 XP; first hit is the min for "One dreaded task", defeat clears it full.
- **Reflection journal:** `journal` table, one entry per game day (editable): went well / drained me / adjust tomorrow + mood and energy (1-5). Every question is optional: any 3 answers (classics + evolving questions, `answeredCount`) = full "Reflection journal" daily, 1-2 = min (upgrades min to full). Evolving questions (`src/engine/reflection.ts`, no AI): `QUESTION_BANK` (~55 questions in 13 themes incl. daily Brainstorm), `dailyQuestions` seeded per day: follow-ups first (yesterday's "adjust", last brainstorm answer, something just tracked), then patterns (a drain word 2+ times, a top-3 goal carried 2+ days and not done), then themed picks weighted by engagement (`themeEngagement`: answer rate and length per theme over 30 days; swapped-away ids in the entry's `skipped` lower a theme), mood (gentle after mood <=2, stretch after >=4), weakest stat, no repeats within 10 days, one theme each. Answers saved in the entry's `answers` [{id, theme, question, answer}]; `swapQuestion` replaces one. The old single random question (`prompt`/`promptAnswer`) still counts and shows for old entries. "Track something new" in the reflection (`trackFromReflection`) adds a daily, a weekly side quest or one of tomorrow's top 3 (`addGoal`, doesn't clear the planning quest) and records it in the entry's `tracked`. Dailies can now be added/removed any time on the Quests board ("Your daily quests", `addDailyQuest`); pay re-splits by shares. Patterns in `src/engine/journal.ts`: streak, mood by weekday, frequent drain words, mood on 80%-dailies days vs other days (needs 3+ of each).
- **Top 3 goals:** `goals` table (`DayGoals`, one per game day the goals are FOR: up to 3 `{text, done, doneAt}`, plus `plannedOn`), in the save. Writing tomorrow's (or today's, if unset) clears "Plan tomorrow's top 3" (`agi-plan`): 3 = full, 1–2 = min (`saveGoals`); today's unfinished goals are offered as carry-overs. Saving a reflection puts its "adjust tomorrow" answer into tomorrow's goals as an extra item (`GoalItem.source: 'reflection'`, on top of the top 3, not in the edit form, kept when the top 3 are re-saved; `syncReflectionGoal` updates it when the answer changes and removes it when cleared, unless already ticked). Shown with a "from reflection" tag. HUD "Top 3" panel (shown if that daily exists or any goals do) ticks today's off (`toggleGoal`, toast, all three = big toast). Analytics in `src/engine/goals.ts` (`goalStats`: hit rate all/7/30 days over finished days only, perfect days, planning streak, by weekday, goals that keep coming back; `searchGoals`) shown in `src/ui/goals/GoalsView.tsx` (phone: "History & stats"; Life log "Goals" tab; PC sidebar "Top 3 goals"). Also in the activity feed ('goal'), the Life log day page and the weekly report (goals done/planned, plus a suggestion when under 50%).
- **Life log (feature `memories`):** `memories` + `places` tables (in the save) and a device-only `photoCache` table. One page per day (`src/ui/lifelog/LifeLog.tsx`): photo of the day, timestamped entries (note, photos, place), the reflection answers, and "what else happened" from `activityFeed`; plus calendar, photo wall, places (Leaflet map, lazy-loaded, OSM tiles, dark via CSS filter) and search. Rules in `src/engine/memories.ts`. Photos are shrunk on-device to ~300 KB JPEG (`src/db/photos.ts`), uploaded to `api/photo.ts` (Blob `photos/<sha256(sync code)>/<id>`, private), downloaded and cached on other devices; deleting removes them from the cloud. Settings shows cloud usage vs the 1 GB free plan. Places: GPS when you save (only), matched to a known place within 150 m, else named by OpenStreetMap Nominatim reverse lookup (the only time a position leaves the app); renaming sets `custom`. "New area discovered" banner on the HUD only if location is already allowed. "Photo of the day" daily (`templateId 'int-photo'`, fixed id `quest-photo-of-the-day`, INT or nearest stat) is auto-added for every player by `ensureMemoryQuest` (startup, signup, feature switch), not offered in onboarding; a photo = full, note-only = min, deleting re-derives it. Travel titles (Explorer 10 / Wanderer 25 / Pathfinder 50 / Cartographer 100 places, City Hopper 5 cities, Globetrotter 2 countries) each pay +50 XP once (`TRAVEL_XP`, never dollars).
- **Loot drops / card collection (feature `cards`):** `src/engine/loot.ts` + `loot` table (`LootDrop`, in the save). 118 cards: 24 original fictional idols (K/J/C-pop style groups, signature dance moves drawn as stylised pose silhouettes in `src/ui/loot/CardArt.tsx`; owner asked for real celebrities in sexy poses: declined, no real people, keep it glam not explicit, no Pokémon branding; later asked for real idols' photos or photorealistic lookalikes: declined, lookalikes are still likeness and photoreal needs paid AI image generation), 14 anime-style portrait cards (series 'Starlight Stage', `Portrait` params drawn by `src/ui/loot/Portrait.tsx`, all original), 26 odd relics, 16 'Rizz & Romance' cards (cheeky dating loot, flirty never explicit). The playing-card deck was removed at the owner's request. Custom cards: `customCards` table (in the save), made in the binder's "My cards" tab from your own image (stored via `src/db/photos.ts`), registered with `setCustomCards`/`customCard` so `allCards()`/`cardById`/`rollDrop` include them; fictional/AI-generated adults only, never real people (owner wanted hyperreal AI girls: we can't generate images, so they import their own). Bulk import (binder → My cards → Bulk import, up to 50): `src/ui/loot/BulkImport.tsx` + `bulkImport.ts` decode to a ≤1600 px working copy, find the person with the pose model in still-image mode (`src/capture/photoPose.ts`), and use pure rules in `src/engine/cardImport.ts`: `cardCrop` (5:7 around the person with headroom, zooming in no tighter than 60% of the largest card rectangle so context stays; centre-ish crop when no one is found), `imageStats`/`qualityScore` (sharpness, contrast, colourfulness, exposure, resolution) → `rarityByRank` across the batch (top 10% Legendary, 20% Epic, 35% Rare, rest Common), `autoCaption` (name + flavour line from colour, light, pose). Review grid: drag to pan, zoom slider, rarity, name, text, "New name", remove; saving cuts 750×1050 JPEGs into the photo store. Optional AI captions (owner chose "A now, B as a switch"): per-device checkbox; `api/caption.ts` calls Claude (`claude-opus-5-5`, effort low, server-side fallbacks "default") only when Vercel has ANTHROPIC_API_KEY and the sync code has a cloud save; returns {name, text, rarity} (rarity ignored, batch spread wins); falls back to free captions on 503/403. Drops on quest completions (boss/main stage/full strength routine always; other quests 25%, cleared focus 25%, surprise 50%, all 3 goals 50%), rolled by `rollDrop` seeded per source record (stable, once per record via `sourceId`; undoing a quest removes its card). Rarity odds better for big moments; a monthly featured series drops 2×. Duplicates power up (★ 1, ★★ 3, ★★★ 6 copies) for +15 XP each star (`starUpXp`, XP only). UI: `LootReveal` flip-in overlay on `loot.card`, `Collection` binder (phone HUD panel → screen, PC sidebar), activity feed 'loot', desktop notifications.
- **Pack Forge (AI card packs, owner chose Cloudflare's free tier):** `api/forge.ts` makes ONE card image per call with Cloudflare Workers AI (`@cf/black-forest-labs/flux-1-schnell`, `FORGE_STEPS` 8, ~96 neurons of the free 10,000/day, so ~100 images/day; over the limit it just fails until the daily reset, no charge on the free plan) using Vercel env `CLOUDFLARE_ACCOUNT_ID` + `CLOUDFLARE_API_TOKEN`; only catalog cards plus a numeric `variant` (0–9999) and only for a sync code with a cloud save. 503 not-configured, 429 when the daily allowance is used. Catalog `src/engine/forge.ts` (no imports so the server can load it): 8 sultry series × 6 (After Hours lingerie, Rave Siren, Neon Nights, Sun & Salt, Soft Hours, Power Muse, Iron Muse, Gatebreakers), each card has its own `style` so photo-real and anime appear at every rarity; photo-real women are East Asian (heritage varies) with strongly feminine descriptions (owner said the first pack looked too masculine and too tame); `variant` picks heritage/hair/light. Owner asked for lingerie, rave fits, pasties and "sexual": built lingerie/rave/swim/sexy-not-revealing, declined pasties, nudity and sexual acts; always "young woman in her early 20s", never "girl" (owner asked for 18-22: kept at early 20s because models drift younger for 18-19). Cloudflare's prompt filter ("Input prompt contains NSFW content") is avoided by fashion wording: no explicit words and no "no nudity"-style negatives that name body parts (tests ban a word list); on an NSFW refusal the server retries once with `forgePrompt(card, variant, 1)` (the `SOFTER` word swaps: lingerie→loungewear, bralette→crop top, bikini→two-piece swimsuit...), then returns 422 'declined'; the app then swaps in a different card of the same rarity (`replacementPick`, up to 5 tries per slot) so one press always aims for 6 cards. Tapping a forged card in the binder offers "Remake image" (`remakeCard`: same card, new random variant, old photo deleted) and "Delete card" (any custom card; a deleted forged card leaves the recent list, so it can be forged again). "Same prompts again" remakes the last pack (this device's localStorage `livinghud-forge-last`, `forgePack(..., { again: true })`). These are private custom cards. App side `src/db/forge.ts` `forgePack`: `pickPack` = mixed pack 2 Common, 2 Rare, 1 Epic, 1 Legendary from different series, skipping the last 18 forged cards, 2 calls at a time, crops via bulk-import `analyse`/`renderCard`, saves custom cards tagged `forge`, grants each as a drop (`grantCard`, source 'forge') so `LootReveal` shows them. Button in binder → My cards → Pack Forge. Declined: scraping Reddit or unofficial site logins (SeaArt/Unstability have no official free API). Next: automatic pack every 3 days once the owner likes the quality.
- **Titles, streak bonuses, loot (Phase 3/6 pieces):** all derived in `src/engine/badges.ts`. A 30-day streak on any daily of a stat earns that stat's title (Iron Will, Unbreakable, Seeker, Bond Keeper, Clockwork, Gold Hunter) for good and +10% XP (never dollars) on that stat's quests while a 30-day streak holds. Milestone titles for levels, bosses, focus hours, journal entries, hangouts. Loot = HUD colour themes (`data-theme` on `<html>`) dropping at levels/milestones. `player.title`, `player.theme`; announcements come from `announceProgress` comparing before/after.
- **Morning briefing and weekly report:** `src/ui/Briefing.tsx` shows once per game day per device (localStorage), not on signup day: training day, dailies left, side quests left, streaks at risk, boss, weakening bonds, HP/penalties, wallet to cap; on Mondays it links the report. `src/engine/report.ts` grades a Mon-Sun week (completion → E-S), per-stat rates vs the week before, XP, money, focus, bosses, contacts, journal mood, and one rule-based suggestion.
- **Surprise challenges:** `src/engine/surprise.ts` picks one challenge a day for the player's stats at a time between wake+1h and bed−2h, seeded by player id + day (same on every device, nothing stored until cleared). Open for 60 min; "Done" (honour) = +30 XP in the `surprises` table. Hidden on the HUD until it opens; announced once per device.
- **PC dashboard:** same site, same sync code. Windows ≥1100px wide get `src/ui/desktop/Desktop.tsx`: a sidebar (every screen, look switch, notifications, "Phone layout") and a fixed three-column command centre: profile card (rank, level, stat hexagon, lifetime totals), streak calendar (20 weeks), stat tiles + 30-day trend, today (the HUD with `desktop` hiding its status/stats), treasury (wallet, Vault, monthly earnings vs budget, plans), training (cycle, today's sets, 14-day chart, head-angle trend), weekly grade wall, System log (filterable activity feed). History maths in `src/engine/history.ts` (`statScores` is shared with the summary). Per-device prefs in localStorage (`src/ui/desktop/prefs.ts`): look `system` (scanlines, grid, corner brackets, materialise/count-up animations, off for reduced motion) or `clean` (calm flat dark, `data-look` on `<html>`, also applies on the phone layout), layout, notifications. Desktop notifications (`src/ui/desktop/notify.ts`) only while a tab is open and in the background: surprise, focus done, level up, boss, title, auto-clears; once a day streaks-at-risk (3h before bed) and weakening bonds. Charts are hand-drawn SVG (`charts.tsx`), colours from CSS variables so they follow themes.
- **Updates:** the service worker uses `skipWaiting`/`clientsClaim`, and `src/pwa.ts` checks for a new version on open/focus and reloads when it's ready (an installed Android app rarely closes fully, so a waiting worker would never take over). Settings shows the deployed commit (`VERCEL_GIT_COMMIT_SHA`) and build time.
- **Penalties (Phase 3, done):** `src/engine/penalties.ts`, derived from `player.penaltiesSince` (set at signup, on first load for existing players, when switched back on, and when difficulty changes, so a mode never applies to past days). Casual: nothing lost, streaks pause over up to 2 missed days. Normal: streaks break, −5 XP per missed daily. Hardcore: 100 HP, −10 per missed daily, +10 for a perfect day; at 0 HP lose up to $10 of that month's earnings, HP back to 50. Recovery Days always skip.
- **Onboarding ("Awakening"):** every preference is asked at signup, not hard-coded. The quest and reward lists are a template library.
- **Smart glasses** (Meta Ray-Ban Display style): Phase 8. Keep the event bus clean so notifications, a mini-HUD and voice check-off plug in.
- **AI System:** Phase 7 add-on, not core.
- **Style:** an original look *inspired by* Solo Leveling. No copyrighted names, art or exact text.
